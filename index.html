The founder

Racheal Allen didn't wait for permission.

She became a mother at 17. She earned a full scholarship to Marygrove College and graduated with a 4.0. By 21, she had launched her first startup, a Farmers Insurance agency, and became a top-producing agent within a year.

Over the next twenty years, she launched startups in legal services, beauty, real estate, and consulting. She led operations for the Education Achievement Authority and the Downtown Detroit Ambassador Program. She became the founding Chief Operating Officer of the Marygrove Conservancy, stewarding a 53-acre campus backed by a $100 million Kresge Foundation investment.

In 2019, she saw a gap no one else was filling. Plenty of organizations taught entrepreneurs how to start a business. Almost no one taught them how to run one. So she founded Operations School. Since then, over 1,000 BIPOC startup and nonprofit leaders have learned to get their businesses legit, with systems, SOPs, and technology that actually work.

In 2022, she co-founded Centric Place with her husband Gerrard, a 10,000 square foot hub for Black arts, culture, and entrepreneurship in Farmington Hills. In 2026, Operations School received a $2.8 million grant from the Michigan Economic Development Corporation to expand nationally, the only Black woman among 27 grantees statewide.
