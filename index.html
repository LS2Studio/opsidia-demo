Meet Your Consultant

Racheal Allen

Racheal is a Lean Six Sigma certified strategy, systems, and operations consultant with nearly twenty years of experience leading and serving startups and nonprofits.

In 2019, she created Operations School, a nonprofit that has trained over 1,000 BIPOC startup and nonprofit leaders. In 2022, she co-founded Centric Place, a first-of-its-kind hub for Black arts, culture, and entrepreneurship in Metro Detroit.

She previously served as the founding Chief Operating Officer for the Marygrove Conservancy, a 53-acre educational campus representing The Kresge Foundation's single largest investment, with a $100 million commitment.

How We Work

A proven process, tailored to you

Every organization is different. Our process adapts to your needs, your timeline, and your goals, while bringing the rigor of Lean Six Sigma to every engagement.

01

Discover

We start by understanding your vision, your challenges, and your goals. Deep listening, honest assessment, no jargon.

02

Design

We co-create a plan with clear outcomes, timelines, and metrics. You know exactly what you're getting and when.

03

Build

We implement systems, processes, and structures. Hands-on support, not just a report that sits on a shelf.

04

Scale

We help you sustain and scale what we've built together, with training, documentation, and ongoing support.
